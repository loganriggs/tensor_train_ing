## Open findings (owner)
1. **`tnlm/train.py` is 1,367 lines and does five jobs (Codex).** It holds the training loop, the spend
   ledger/stage budgets, lagged calibration, held-out evaluation, mechanism evaluation and the summary. Suggested split:
   `budget.py` (SpendLedger, stage admission), `calibration.py`, `evaluate.py` (evaluate, compare_evaluations,
   evaluate_mechanisms), leaving `train.py` as the loop. Pure moves, re-exported from `train` for compatibility.
2. **`tnlm/data.py` is 819 lines and includes the synthetic mechanism tasks (Codex).** Move them to `tnlm/tasks.py`.
3. **The reference model is CPU-only by convention (Claude).** `attention_backend: auto` silently switches to Triton
   on CUDA. That's documented in config, but `validate_config` should print the resolved backend.
4. **Test fixtures are duplicated (both).** `TINY` shapes appear in test_math, test_optim and test_kernels; Codex tests
   import fixtures from `test_train`. Move shared fixtures into `tests/conftest.py` and stop importing test modules from tests.
5. **Coordination log (both).** `COORDINATION.md` is now ~100 lines of append-only notes. Keep its top part
   (ownership, interfaces, status) current and archive resolved request threads into `docs/reviews/`.
6. **Analysis-only state on modules (Claude).** `Block.ablate` and `_DynamicNorm.denom_mode` are plain attributes,
   reset by a context manager in `analysis.py`. That's fine, but a training-time assertion that both are at their
   defaults would catch leakage cheaply.
