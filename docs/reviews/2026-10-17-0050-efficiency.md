## Repeated bug class: disk projections that miss a term
Three refusals so far came from retention settings. This time it would have been **kept finals accumulating across a sweep**, which
`check_experiment_configs.py` didn't model. Its "×2 for cooldown" rule was a guess, and it assumed 16 B/param. Fixed:
- `--keep-count K` adds K kept cooled finals to the projection.
- Per-state bytes now come from the trainer's own figure (12 B/param = FP32 params + 2 Adam moments, matching
  `checkpoint_storage_plan.per_checkpoint_estimate_bytes`). Peak = parent staging + one cooldown state, as in the real sweep journal.
- Lesson: derive projections from the tool's own planner output (`sweep.json` `*_storage_plan`), not from my own estimates.
  That's the third time an estimate of mine has been wrong where the tool's number was right.
