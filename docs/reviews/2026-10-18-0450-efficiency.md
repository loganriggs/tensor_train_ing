## Repeated pattern: confounds introduced by shrinking a test
Three of the four canary cycles were partly confounded by the test being smaller than the real run:
1. **width 64 / 1 head**: an init blow-up that doesn't occur at width 512 (random-token check);
2. **random tokens vs real text**: random ids hid the repeated-token outliers that actually break `lagged` (real-data calibration showed them);
3. **token-denominated half-life**: at 512 vs 8192 tokens/update, lagged's 1e6-token half-life became ~1950 updates (frozen) instead of ~122.

**Changes adopted:**
- `norm_canary.py` now **rescales token-denominated settings automatically** (lagged half-life × 512/base tokens-per-update).
- **Rule:** when shrinking a test, (a) use real data, never random tokens, for anything involving attention scores; (b) list every
  setting expressed in tokens or absolute counts (half-lives, warmup, milestones, probes) and rescale it to keep its per-update meaning;
  (c) confirm a failure at the target shape (one calibration step at 12×512 took ~10 min) before concluding.
