## Lesson: the recipe checklist was missing throughput
`spectral_rms` passed every checklist item (loss on 2 seeds, LR optimum, compatibility, 1B stability, analysis cost) and then turned out to be **11%
slower**. That's material for a fixed GPU budget, and I found it only because I checked tok/s at the last step. Also, the 100M gain (−0.025) didn't
carry over to 1B at the base LR (+0.009, a tie). The checklist's 1B requirement was right, and its result changed the recommendation.
**Change adopted:** checklist item (6), throughput at the target width on the same GPU, measured from the run's own metrics.
