## Lesson: a cheap high-upside test waited ~19 h behind incumbent refinement
The Muon transfer test (2.3 GPU-hours) was queued at 11:15 yesterday, **behind** the AdamW width-exponent fit (7.5 h) and other AdamW/pre_rms
refinements. It turned out to be worth more than all of them combined (−0.14 vs the ~0.01–0.03 effects being refined). The coordinate check
had already shown spectral Muon flattening the update slopes, which was a strong prior signal that I didn't weigh in queue ordering.
**Rule adopted: order the queue by expected value per GPU-hour.** Cheap tests of an *alternative* with a large plausible effect (here suggested
by a CPU signal) go before long refinements of the incumbent. Concretely, any ≤3 GPU-hour test whose plausible effect is ≥5× the effects
being refined jumps the queue.
