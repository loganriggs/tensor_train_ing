## Repeated issue: quoting a plan before checking its infrastructure assumptions
The 14:40 cost answer assumed (a) multi-budget cooldown branches from one parent and (b) per-width throughput. I verified (a) only afterwards
(supported via `project.milestone_tokens` + `tnlm cooldown`; one constant LR per size, ≤0.01 CE). For (b) my one-liner picked the *first* run
per width, which was noisy (w1024 17.8k vs a median of 32k). Both held up, but by luck. **Rule: before giving a cost or plan, check each
infrastructure assumption (one grep or one command), or label it unverified in the answer.** I did label (a) as "to confirm", which was the right
fallback.
