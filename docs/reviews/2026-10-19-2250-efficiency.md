## The costly miss: an invariant nobody measured
`input_tokens_seen` has been in every `result.json` since the trainer was written; dividing it by `tokens` reveals the 1.96× read in one line.
It went unexamined for ~3 days and ~200 runs, and silently corrupted `lr_horizon` (the H100 LR rule). The general lesson: **for each resource
a run consumes (data, steps, memory, time), compare what was consumed against what was intended, automatically.** Throughput had a watcher;
data consumption had none.

## Changes adopted now

  the red-team finding (lr_horizon 1.89 epochs; joint_transfer 1.93). It joins the orphan finder in every code-health review.
