## Trap caught before it mattered
The re-fit pattern `h(?P<group>\d+)m_lr…` matches both the new `lr_horizon2` runs and the old data-repeating `lr_horizon` 300M runs (same names).
`collect` keeps the newest file per (budget, LR), so the overlapping LRs come from the new set, but the old 2.25e-4 point (no new counterpart) would
silently stay in the fit. **Change:** `fit_lr_optimum.py --exclude REGEX` (+ test); the re-fit is
`fit_lr_optimum.py --pattern 'h(?P<group>\d+)m_lr(?P<lr>[0-9.e-]+)_s101' --also --exclude '^lr_horizon/.*h300m'`.

**Preliminary (3 of 4 points; do not apply yet):** 300M optimum ≈ 4.3e-4 (old, repeated-data fit: 5.7e-4). With the valid 50M points (6.8e-4), the
horizon exponent is ≈ −0.26 rather than −0.10. Extrapolated 560× to 28B tokens, that's a ~2.8× lower H100 LR than the configs use. A 2-budget
power law over that range is fragile, so the next opportunity is a third horizon point.
